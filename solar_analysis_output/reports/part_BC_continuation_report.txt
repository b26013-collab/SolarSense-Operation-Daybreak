4. Outliers: the IQR rule flags 1965 of 32686 non-missing radiation readings; the lower and upper fences are -528.277 and 883.742. Inspect data/radiation_iqr_outliers.csv for timestamps and accompanying environmental values. Possible explanations include cloud transitions, cloud-edge brightening, low solar angle, or sensor issues; the IQR rule alone cannot determine the cause.



BONUS INSIGHT AND LIMITATIONS

Hourly peak comparison: radiation peaks at 12:00 and temperature peaks at 12:00, a difference of +0 hours. A later temperature peak is consistent with thermal lag, although hourly aggregation and seasonal coverage may affect the estimate. Verify the pattern in graphs/A2_mean_radiation_by_hour.png and the temperature plots.
