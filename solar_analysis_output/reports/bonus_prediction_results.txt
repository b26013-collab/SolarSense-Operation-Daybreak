Interpretation: time-only is the forecast-style model; weather-only shows weather information alone; combined estimates the benefit of both. Temperature is partly downstream of radiation and makes the combined model a nowcast if temperature is not known at forecast time.
ClearSky is an idealised half-sine envelope based on a dataset percentile, not a validated atmospheric clear-sky model. ClearnessIdx and physics-informed predictions inherit that limitation.
